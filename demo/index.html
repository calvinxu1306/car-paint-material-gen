Car paint from one photo

A network looks at a single flash photo of car paint and estimates its layers: the base coat's colour, roughness and metalness, how strong and how large its metallic flakes are, and how glossy its clear coat is. These samples were held out from training.

Greyed rows were not recovered: the network does no better than guessing the average. The likely reason is the capture setup — in a dark room the clear coat reflects only the flash, leaving almost no trace of its strength or ripple. The network also estimates how flaky the paint is, but not which way each individual flake points, so the predicted flake map can look smoother than the true one.
